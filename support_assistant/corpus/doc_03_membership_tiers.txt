Zepto Basic membership is free. Zepto Pass costs INR 49 per month and provides free standard delivery and 5% off on select products. Zepto Pass+ costs INR 99 per month and provides free priority delivery, 10% off on select products, and early access to offers for 24 hours. Membership can be cancelled at any time, which stops the next billing cycle, but there is no refund for the current membership period.
