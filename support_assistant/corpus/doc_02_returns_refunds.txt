Grocery and perishable items must be reported for return within 24 hours if they are damaged, spoiled, or incorrect. Non-perishable items can be returned within 7 days if they are unopened and resalable. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days or can be provided instantly to the Zepto wallet. Opened personal care products are non-returnable except in cases of manufacturing defects. Free pickup is provided where required.
